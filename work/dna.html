3D Modeling, Retopology, Texturing, Trailer Editing
My work on DNA of Italian Motorsport centered on the 3D realization of the cars featured in the experience, with a strong focus on optimizing each model for photorealistic visualization and full compatibility with Reality Composer Pro.

I was also responsible for the CGI scenes of the project's trailer, developing the camera direction so that each shot would adapt smoothly to the transitions between the different scenes.
