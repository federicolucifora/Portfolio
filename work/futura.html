Futura
Futura is the metaverse created by Plenitude. It is a multi-platform project where my role was to create and texture assets for the environment. I also worked on environment design for some levels in Unity and Roblox Studio. Additionally, a 3D representation of the Futura World was created, and I contributed by designing some of the continents along with their respective materials and textures.
