DNA of Italian Motorsport
DNA of Italian Motorsport is an immersive experience created for ACI that reveals the history, circuits, vehicles and culture behind Italian racing. The ACI logo becomes a conversational AI guide, leading visitors through archival footage, stereoscopic sequences, animated dioramas and full-scale vehicle reconstructions that together form a spatial atlas of the sport.

3D Modeling, Retopology, Texturing, Trailer Editing
The journey begins with the ACI logo becoming a conversational character. Visitors speak to it naturally, and the experience responds by guiding them toward the period, theme or story closest to their curiosity. AI becomes the interface that gives structure to a modular body of archive materials and spatial content.

Around the visitor, glassy screens present archival films, interviews, diagrams and historical documents. Each period combines different spatial media: stereoscopic archive sequences, animated dioramas of iconic Italian racetracks, high-resolution in-car VR180 footage and full-scale reconstructions of representative vehicles.

Together, these materials create a spatial atlas of Italian motorsport, where visitors can follow their own curiosity through the culture behind the race.
